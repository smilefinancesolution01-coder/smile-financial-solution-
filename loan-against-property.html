ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply For A Loan Against Property?

Eligibility may vary from lender to lender. Factors such as income, property value, repayment capacity and credit profile are commonly considered during assessment.

Salaried Individuals

Employees working with government, private companies, PSUs and multinational organizations may be eligible subject to lender requirements.

Self-Employed Professionals

Doctors, Chartered Accountants, Architects, Consultants and other professionals may apply based on income and property ownership.

Business Owners

Proprietorship firms, partnership businesses, LLPs and private limited companies may seek funding against eligible properties.

Property Owners

Applicants should generally have ownership of an eligible residential, commercial or industrial property acceptable to the lender.

PROPERTY ASSESSMENT

How Property Evaluation Works

Before approving a loan, lenders generally conduct a detailed review of the property being offered as security for financing.

Property Verification

Ownership documents and property records are reviewed to verify authenticity and eligibility.

Market Value Assessment

A valuation process helps determine the estimated market value of the property.

Legal Review

Legal checks may be conducted to examine title, ownership history and documentation status.

Final Eligibility Assessment

Income profile, repayment capacity and property value are evaluated before final decision-making.
