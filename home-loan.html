ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Apply For A Home Loan?

Home loans may be available to eligible salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, business owners and other qualifying applicants. Eligibility is generally assessed based on income profile, repayment capacity, employment history, property details and supporting documentation.

Salaried Individuals

Employees working in private organizations, public sector institutions and government departments may be eligible subject to profile assessment and documentation requirements.

Self-Employed Professionals

Doctors, consultants, architects, chartered accountants and other professionals may apply based on business stability and income records.

Business Owners

Proprietors, partners and company directors may explore home financing opportunities based on business performance and financial documentation.
