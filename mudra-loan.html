Who Can Apply?

Mudra Loan generally small businesses, self-employed individuals aur micro enterprises ke liye designed hai. Actual eligibility lender policies ke hisab se vary kar sakti hai.

Shop Owners

Retail shops, kirana stores aur local trading businesses.

Service Providers

Salon, repair centers, coaching institutes aur service-based businesses.

Manufacturing Units

Small manufacturing businesses aur production units.

Traders

Wholesalers, distributors aur trading related businesses.

Self Employed Professionals

Independent professionals aur business operators.

New Entrepreneurs

Individuals jo apna naya business start karna chahte hain.

Documents Commonly Required

Applicant profile aur lender requirements ke according additional documents bhi maange ja sakte hain.

Identity Proof

Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID ya other valid identity documents.

Address Proof

Aadhaar Card, Electricity Bill, Passport ya other acceptable address proof.

Business Proof

Bank Statements

Recent banking transactions aur account statements.

Photographs

Recent passport size photographs as required.

Additional Financial Documents

Income records, quotations ya business related supporting documents where applicable.
