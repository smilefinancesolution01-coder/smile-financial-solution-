ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Who Can Apply For A Personal Loan?

Personal loans may be available to eligible salaried individuals, self-employed professionals and business owners, subject to profile assessment, documentation and lender-specific requirements.

Salaried Individuals

Employees working in private companies, public sector organizations and government departments may explore personal financing options depending on eligibility requirements.

Self-Employed Professionals

Doctors, architects, consultants, freelancers and other professionals may apply based on their income profile and supporting documents.

Business Owners

Business owners seeking funds for personal requirements may be eligible subject to assessment and documentation.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

Important Documents For Application

Document requirements may vary depending on the applicant profile and financing institution.

Identity Proof

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Passport (if available)
  • Voter ID Card
  • Driving Licence

Address Proof

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Electricity Bill
  • Passport
  • Rental Agreement
  • Utility Bills

Income Proof

  • Salary Slips
  • Bank Statements
  • Income Tax Returns
  • Form 16 (if applicable)
  • Professional Income Records
